Levant Desk Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Levant Desk is operated by George Yammine ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Levant Desk text-message line at (415) 625-5596.

What we collect

When you text or call the Levant Desk number we receive your phone number, the content of your messages, and the time they were sent. If you book a class, we keep the class and time you chose.

How we use it

We use this information only to answer your questions, confirm and remind you about classes you book, and follow up about them. Replies may be drafted by an automated assistant and are approved by a person before they are sent.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We use service providers to run the line (a messaging carrier and a hosting provider) only to deliver and store messages for the purposes above.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages. Reply HELP for help. Msg & data rates may apply.

Deletion

To have your number and messages deleted, email admin@gyammine.com.

Contact

George Yammine, operating as Levant Desk — admin@gyammine.com. See also our Terms.
