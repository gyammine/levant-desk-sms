Program

Levant Desk, operated by George Yammine, is a text-message front desk for a gym. When you text or call (415) 625-5596, we reply to your questions about classes, schedule and prices; if you book a class, we send a confirmation, reminders before it, a check-in after it, and up to two follow-ups about continuing.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with your conversation; after a booking, expect up to about 6 messages over two weeks. Message and data rates may apply.

Help and opting out

Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out of all messages, including scheduled reminders. Reply START to opt back in.
